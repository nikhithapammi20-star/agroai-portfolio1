01 / About the project

Better farming starts with better information.

AgroAI is a student portfolio concept for a smart farming platform. It illustrates how artificial intelligence, connected sensors, and a simple web dashboard could help farmers understand field conditions and make timely decisions.

The goal is practical: use water thoughtfully, notice crop issues earlier, and make farm data easier to understand—even for first-time technology users.

03 / How it works

Four steps from field to insight.

A simple system flow makes the project easier to explain, prototype, and improve.

01

Collect

Gather field readings such as soil moisture, temperature, and crop photos.

02

Analyze

Use rules or trained AI models to interpret patterns and identify potential concerns.

03

Recommend

Translate the analysis into understandable tips and alerts for the user.

04

Improve

Review trends over time and refine the system with field feedback.
